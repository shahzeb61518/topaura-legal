TopAura Privacy Policy

App: TopAura — Status Bar Effects (com.orbix.topaurastatusbareffects) · Effective 21 October 2026

In short: TopAura does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers. Everything it does happens on your phone.

What TopAura does

TopAura draws decorative light effects (glow, petals, snow, a charging line and a notification pulse) along the top edge of your screen. Your effect settings are saved only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Permissions and why they are used

PermissionPurpose
Display over other appsDraws the effect strip on top of other apps. The strip ignores touches and cannot see what is on screen.
Foreground service & notificationsKeeps the effects running and shows the “TopAura is on” notification with a Turn off button.
Notification access (optional)Used only for the notification pulse. TopAura checks that a notification arrived, whether it is silent, and the app’s accent colour. It never reads, stores or transmits the title, text, sender or any other content of your notifications.
Run at startupTurns the effects back on after a restart if you had them on.
Battery statusRead on the device to draw the charging line. Not stored or shared.

Data collection and sharing

TopAura collects no data. No information is sent off your device, and no third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising or crash reporting are included. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play under Google’s Privacy Policy; TopAura never sees your payment details.

Children

TopAura is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
